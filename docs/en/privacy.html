Privacy

In short: Atramentum collects no data. Your text, characters, notes and settings stay on your devices. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

The app

  • Your projects are stored only where you choose: on your device or a drive of your choice. Atramentum does not transfer them anywhere.
  • Syncing between devices uses a folder you choose yourself, for example in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive. Atramentum only places change packages there. Those services have their own privacy policies. Without sync set up, nothing leaves your device.
  • Spelling check, read aloud and dictation use the services of your operating system. Dictation on the Mac recognises speech on the device when macOS supports this for the chosen language; otherwise Apple's speech recognition takes over. On iPhone, iPad and Android, your system keyboard determines how dictation works.
  • Updates: The App Store version gets updates through the App Store. The GitHub versions ask GitHub at startup whether a new version exists. As with any website visit, GitHub sees your IP address. Atramentum sends no other information.
  • Links (report a problem, donate) open your browser. A connection to GitHub or Ko-fi is only made when you click such a link.
  • Tips in the App Store: Purchases are handled by Apple. Atramentum only learns that a purchase succeeded.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address when you visit (see GitHub's privacy statement). To show the download count, the page fetches public information from GitHub in your browser. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers.

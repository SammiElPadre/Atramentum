ATRAMENTUM FOR ANDROID – INSTALLATION (TEST RELEASE)
====================================================

Atramentum is free. There is no paid version, no account and no sign-up.
Atramentum does not need the internet and never sends your texts or
personal data anywhere. Only if you allow it does it check GitHub for a
new version, at most once a day.

Requirements: Android 7 or later, phone or tablet.

Atramentum for Android is not in the Play Store but available directly
here on GitHub. That's why Android asks for your permission once.

This is a test release. Please make regular backups
(File › Create Backup …) and report anything you notice.


1. Install
----------
1. Open this page on your Android device and tap the file
   "Atramentum_<version>_android.apk".
2. After the download, tap "Open" (or tap the file in the "Files" app
   under "Downloads").
3. Android says your browser is not allowed to install apps:
   tap "Settings", turn on "Allow from this source", go back, then tap
   "Install".
4. Google Play Protect may warn that the app is unknown. That is because
   it does not come from the Play Store. Choose "More details" and
   "Install anyway".

Atramentum then appears among your apps.


2. Updates
----------
On first launch, Atramentum asks whether it should look for new versions
itself. You can also choose "Help › Check for Updates …" at any time.
On the first update, Android asks once for permission to "Install apps
from this source" for Atramentum; after that, tapping "Install" is enough.
Your projects are kept.


3. Where your projects are stored
---------------------------------
Projects, exports and backups are stored in the app's protected area.
After creating an export (Word, PDF, Markdown) or a backup, Atramentum
offers it via "Share": save to Files, send by email, put it in
Google Drive …

IMPORTANT: If you uninstall Atramentum, Android also deletes all of the
app's projects. Create a backup first and save it outside the app
(File › Create Backup …, then "Share").


4. Sync with other devices
--------------------------
Google Drive and OneDrive do not create a synced folder on Android. To
sync with a Mac, Windows PC or another device, you need a sync app that
keeps a folder on the device in sync with your cloud (for example
FolderSync or Autosync for Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox, or
Syncthing between your own devices). Choose that folder in Atramentum
under "File › Set Up Sync …".
These apps come from other providers; we have not tested them.


5. Using Atramentum on a phone
------------------------------
- Chapters and scenes: the button on the far left of the bar below the menu.
- "Margin" (scene details, note, characters): the button on the far right.
- Corkboard and character web: zoom and pan with two fingers.
- The back gesture first closes open lists, menus and dialogs.


6. Uninstall
------------
Settings › Apps › Atramentum › Uninstall. Create a backup first
(see section 3).
